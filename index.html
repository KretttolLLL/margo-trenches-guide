3Risk management basics

📏 Position sizing

Risk about 1-2% of your trading wallet per trade. 1 SOL wallet → 0.01-0.02 SOL per play. Lots of small bets beat one big gamble.

💰 Take profits in steps

At 2x, sell enough to take your initial back. Then you're playing with house money. Scale out more at 3x, 5x, 10x.

🚫 Never chase

Missed the entry? Let it go. Green candles you didn't catch aren't losses. Chasing tops is how wallets die.

⚙️ Slippage & priority fee

Slippage around 10-20% for fresh memecoins, lower for bigger ones. Keep priority fee modest (e.g. ~0.001-0.005 SOL); on small trades high fees eat your profit.

4Scam safety

Never share your seed phrase or private key. Not with staff, not with "support", not with anyone. No real team will ever ask for it.
