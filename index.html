2 · How to read Margo Trenches calls

Three tiers.
One rule: small size.

📡

Sniper

Earliest · highest risk · most calls

First signal on a fresh token. Many will fail. Tiny size only.

⚡

Alpha

Smart-money confirmation

More tracked wallets are in. Fewer calls, better odds, still risky.

💠

Apex

Strongest confirmation · fewest calls

Highest conviction setups. Still not guaranteed. Size sensibly.

Every call shows

  • MC at call: the market cap when the signal fired. If it's already 3-5x higher, you are late. Don't chase.
  • CA (contract address): tap to copy, paste it into Axiom / Fomo search. Always use the CA from our channels, never one posted by a random account.
  • Check the chart on DexScreener first: liquidity, volume, holders, is it already pumped?
  • Small size. Start with amounts you're fine losing completely while you learn.

3 · Risk management basics

Small bets.
Long game.

📏

Position sizing

1-2%

Risk about 1-2% of your trading wallet per trade. 1 SOL wallet → 0.01-0.02 SOL per play. Lots of small bets beat one big gamble.

🚫

Never chase

Missed the entry? Let it go. Green candles you didn't catch aren't losses. Chasing tops is how wallets die.

💰

Take profits in steps

2x

At 2x, sell enough to take your initial back. Then you're playing with house money. Scale out more at 3x, 5x, 10x.

⚙️

Slippage & priority fee

Slippage around 10-20% for fresh memecoins, lower for bigger ones. Keep priority fee modest (e.g. ~0.001-0.005 SOL); on small trades high fees eat your profit.

4 · Scam safety

Your keys.
Nobody else's.

Never share your seed phrase or private key.

Not with staff, not with "support", not with anyone. No real team will ever ask for it.

Staff never DMs you first.

Anyone DMing you "from Margo Trenches" offering help, signals or a giveaway is a scammer. Block and report.

Fake airdrops & drainers

"Claim your tokens" sites and random tokens appearing in your wallet are traps. Don't connect your wallet or sign anything you don't understand.

Check the CA from our channels only.

Scammers copy names and tickers. The contract address is what counts.

On rugcheck.xyz: mint/freeze authority, top-holder concentration, locked liquidity.
